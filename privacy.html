1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to BITCHAIN PRO X ("Platform", "We", "Us", or "Our"). We are committed to upholding absolute data integrity, privacy protection, and transparent security standards for all registered members participating in our Web2/Web3 digital package and referral growth ecosystem.

This Privacy Policy outlines how your personal information, login credentials, referral statistics, and wallet configurations are safely processed and stored.

📊
2. Information We Collect

When you register an account or interact with BITCHAIN PRO X, we collect essential account metadata necessary to deliver smooth decentralized access and automated commission tracking:

  • Account Identity Information: Full Name, unique Username, Email Address, and Phone Number with Country Dialing Code.
  • Authentication Credentials: Passwords encrypted using enterprise-grade 256-bit hash functions via Supabase Auth infrastructure.
  • Referral Network Data: Sponsor Username associations and referral link attributions for tier reward calculations.
  • Technical Session Metadata: Device IP, browser identifier, and timestamp session cookies for session persistence.
✦ Zero Unencrypted Storage Guarantee: BITCHAIN PRO X never stores raw plain-text user passwords. All credentials are encrypted end-to-end via secure cryptographic hashes.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is strictly utilized to provide reliable network infrastructure and user account management:

  • Authenticating user sessions and granting access to member dashboard features.
  • Calculating non-working, direct referral, and team bonus rewards accurately.
  • Sending critical account notifications, password reset links, and security alerts.
  • Preventing fraudulent registrations, multi-account abuse, or unauthorized access attempts.
🔐
4. Security & Data Protection Policies

We enforce strict security measures including Row Level Security (RLS) policies within our Supabase database cluster. Your user record can only be accessed or modified by your authenticated session.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing brokers under any circumstances.

5. User Rights & Account Management

Every BITCHAIN PRO X member retains full right to review, update, or request modification of their profile details via the Settings Page. You may also contact our support team to request account deletion or data portability exports.
